Settings and rules
  1. Unicode NFC. Zero-width joiners and non-joiners are removed unless a non-joiner sits between two letters.
  2. Only letters that Urdu, Persian or Arabic keyboards produce instead of Sindhi ones are rewritten: ی ى ے → ي, ہ → ه or ھ, ك → ڪ or ک. These letters are never written into a word.
  3. Where there is a choice, the spelling more frequent in Sindhi Wikipedia wins.
  4. An Urdu keyboard has no ڪ, so ک becomes ڪ only when the typed form is seen at most 100 times and the other at least 50× more often (کي and ڪي are different words).
  5. A word written entirely in Sindhi letters is otherwise left as it is. Pairs a native speaker judged different are never merged.

Measured with the default rules: 94.9% of words altered by Urdu-keyboard typing (ي typed as ی) are restored; 0.03% of clean UD words and 0.002% of clean SiPOS news words change. The 0.1.0 rules restored 94.0% but changed 1.35% and 0.39%.

Same rules and output as the sindhinorm Python package (MIT): pip install sindhinorm. Part of the MorphoTok-SD-UR project. Cite: doi:10.5281/zenodo.24093294. Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere. Word counts: Sindhi Wikipedia, 2023-11-01 (61,718 words seen at least twice), by Wikipedia contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0.
